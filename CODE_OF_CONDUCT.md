# Code of conduct

## Short version

Be kind. Be technically honest. Don't ship surveillance. Don't help anyone extract a signing key or build a mass-exploit for live devices.

## Our standards

Things we want:

- Direct technical feedback, especially when it's uncomfortable.
- Patience with people who are new to Android / Compose / Kotlin / reverse engineering.
- Crediting people whose work you're building on.

Things we don't:

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks.
- Doxxing anyone — including Meta employees whose names might surface in reverse-engineering notes.
- Shipping telemetry / surveillance features, even opt-in-at-install.
- Helping anyone exploit live Quest devices in a way that'd harm their owners.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, PRs, discussions, the (eventual) Discussions tab, any venue where we're acting on behalf of QuestClassic.

## Enforcement

Maintainers remove comments, lock issues, block contributors, and revoke commit access as needed. We won't publish a ladder-of-responses — the right action depends on the incident. If you think a specific decision was wrong, email the maintainers (contact in SUPPORT.md path).
